Jazz & Bar Dolphin is a small jazz bar in Katamachi, Kanazawa. Some nights are live performances or jam sessions; other nights are relaxed bar nights with records, whisky, gin and conversation. You do not need to know jazz to enjoy the room.

How to find us

Dolphin is inside Million Building in Katamachi. The bar is on the 4th floor, so it may not be visible from street level.

Find Million Building
2-13-11 Katamachi, Kanazawa, Ishikawa 920-0981. Use the Google Maps button if you are nearby.
Enter the building and go to 4F
Once inside Million Building, go up to the 4th floor.
Look for Jazz & Bar Dolphin
You have arrived when you find the Dolphin entrance on the 4th floor. If you have trouble finding us, call 080-3895-0821.
Directions in Google Maps Call Dolphin

Arrival photos will be added once the street-to-door route is photographed. Until then, the address and 4F guidance above are the verified public route information.
